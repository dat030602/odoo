Duplicate Customer Detection

Keep your customer database clean and trustworthy. The module detects duplicate customers in real time by Name, Email, Phone and Mobile Phone, and shows an immediate warning banner directly on customer forms — and on every Sales, Purchase, Invoice and CRM document that references them.

Real-time Alerts Cross-Model 4 Detection Rules No Setup

Compatible with Odoo 19 and installed automatically on top of the standard sale, purchase, account, crm and contacts apps.

Key Features

Stop duplicate customers before they pollute your pipeline, your invoices and your reports.

Real-time Alerts

A warning banner appears immediately on the customer form while you edit, pointing to the existing record so you can prevent the duplicate right away.

4 Detection Rules

Detect duplicates by Name, Email, Phone and Mobile Phone. Each match highlights the existing customer and the rule that triggered it.

Cross-Model Coverage

The same duplicate warning is synced to Sales Orders, Purchase Orders, Invoices and CRM Leads, because every one of them references the customer.

Immediate Visibility

The moment a user opens or updates a customer form, the module scans the existing database for matching partners and surfaces any duplicate in an inline warning banner — no manual checks required.

Proactive Protection

Because the warning also travels to every Sales Order, Purchase Order, Invoice and CRM Lead, your sales and finance teams always see the duplicate context before they commit a deal or issue a document.

  • Detects duplicates by Name, Email, Phone and Mobile
  • No configuration required - works out of the box
  • Real-time warning banner on customer forms
  • Warning synced to Sales, Purchases, Invoices and CRM
  • Works seamlessly with standard Odoo partner forms
  • Points directly to the existing duplicate record
