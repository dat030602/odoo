How It Works

Three quick steps to selectively bill your purchase orders.

Step 1

Open Purchase Order

Click the Create Bill button on any confirmed purchase order to open the invoice dialog.

Step 2

Select Desired Lines

Choose the selection mode, review available lines, and tick the checkboxes next to the items to be billed.

Step 3

Create Draft Bill

Click Create Draft. Your vendor bill is generated with only the selected items, ready for validation.
