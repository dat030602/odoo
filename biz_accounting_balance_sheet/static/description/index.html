Accounting Balance Sheet according to Circular 200 of Vietnamese accounting

The module will automatically design reports based on the latest Circular 200 form of the accounting system, including results from main business activities and results from financial and other activities of the enterprise.

Benefits

  • Save a lot of time when making statistics
  • Ensure the accuracy of the calculation results
  • Convenient and simple for individuals and companies.
  • Available in accordance with the latest legal circulars
  • Easy to repair and adjust

The main activities of the module

Create available targets

The system automatically creates available indicators based on the form of Circular 200

Configuration of each indicator

Create sample reports

Users choose the indicators shown on the report depending on the purpose of use and are most suitable for the business

Calculation and report export

  • The system automatically calculates targets according to configuration.
  • Closing the report results.
  • Print the report to an excel file according to form B01-DN circular 200.
