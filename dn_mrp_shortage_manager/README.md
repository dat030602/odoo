# Manufacturing Shortage Manager

## Overview
Shifts the production manager's perspective from a "Work Order" view to a "Component" view. It identifies specific raw materials that are causing bottlenecks across multiple Manufacturing Orders.

## Key Features
* **Component-Centric View:** Groups shortages by raw material, showing exactly how many MOs are stalled due to a specific missing component.
* **ETA & Delay Risk Tracking:** Compares the Expected Arrival Date (from Purchase Orders) against the Scheduled Start Date of the MO. If the material will arrive too late, a Red Risk Banner is displayed.
* **Actionable Insights:** Production planners can immediately identify critical shortages and prioritize expediting specific purchase orders.

## Usage
Navigate to **Manufacturing > Operations > Shortage Dashboard** to generate the real-time shortage report.
