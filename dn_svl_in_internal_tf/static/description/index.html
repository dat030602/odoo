Accounting Gap

The Inter-Warehouse Valuation Problem in Standard Odoo

In native Odoo 19, internal transfers between warehouses, branch stores, or fulfillment centers frequently bypass perpetual stock valuation or produce single lumped journal entries lacking transit account clearing.

Without dn_svl_in_internal_tf

Standard Odoo Internal Transfer Limitations
  • No SVL on Internal Moves: Stock Valuation Layers are completely skipped, hiding physical inventory transfers from valuation reports.
  • Unbalanced Branch Balance Sheets: When transferring goods between branches with separate valuation accounts, direct journal lines fail to trace in-transit inventory.
  • Audit Trail Disconnect: Inventory managers and financial auditors cannot match individual transfer lines to specific general ledger journal entries.
  • Rigid All-or-Nothing System: No granular control to choose which move types (Receipts, Deliveries, Transfers) should automate valuation vs entries.

With dn_svl_in_internal_tf

Automated 2-Step Balanced Valuation
  • Paired SVL Records: Automatically creates paired layers: Source Location OUT (-qty, -value) and Destination Location IN (+qty, +value).
  • 2-Step Transit Accounting: Automatically posts two balanced journal entries through a designated Inter-Warehouse Transit Clearing Account.
  • 1:1 SVL-to-JE Direct Mapping: Outbound SVL links directly to the OUT journal entry; Inbound SVL links directly to the IN journal entry.
  • Granular Move Type Engine: Flexible configuration toggles to selectively activate SVL and JE generation for Deliveries, Receipts, and Transfers.

Customizable Workflow

Movement Type Configuration Engine

Complete company-level control over automated stock valuation and journal entry posting by movement classification.

Automated SVL Move Types

Specify precisely which movement types automatically produce Stock Valuation Layers (auto_svl_move_type_ids): Receipts, Deliveries, or Internal Transfers.

Automated Journal Entry Move Types

Independently govern which movement types automatically post general ledger journal entries (auto_je_move_type_ids), allowing phased rollouts or non-accounting staging warehouses.

Location Valuation Account Fallback

Assign location-specific stock valuation accounts directly on stock.location. Transfers smoothly debit/credit location accounts, falling back to product category defaults when unassigned.
