Completed Stock Move Modification Engine

Need to fix quantity discrepancies on validated stock moves? Modify completed transfers safely while ensuring complete ledger integrity and strict accounting lock date enforcement.

Mode 1 • Create Adjustment Diff

Recommended

Preserves original valuation layers untouched. Creates compensatory adjustment layers and balancing journal entries to record the delta with full audit traceability.

Configurable Cost Basis for Decreases:
  • Current Standard Price: Values the quantity reduction using current catalog cost.
  • Original Layer Cost: Uses the exact historical acquisition cost of the original move.
100% Audit-Proof • No historical data mutation

Mode 2 • Update Existing Layers

Direct In-Place

Directly updates the original SVL record quantities and amounts in-place. Automatically recalculates and updates linked journal entry lines to maintain balance.

Strict Lock Date Verification:

  • Verifies Tax Lock Date to block tampering with closed periods.

Smart Architecture

Multi-Tier Valuation Account Resolution

Eliminate accounting bottlenecks. The engine determines the exact stock valuation account through intelligent hierarchical lookups across products, categories, and warehouse locations:

Tier 1 • Location-Specific

Location Valuation Account

Configure dn_valuation_account_id directly on any warehouse or internal location for specific physical inventory segregation.

Tier 2 • Parent Location

Hierarchical Lookup

If not specified on the bin or rack, seamlessly walks up parent nodes (Sub-shelf → Rack → Zone → Main Warehouse) to inherit accounts.

Tier 3 • Category Fallback

Product Category & Switch

Smoothly falls back to Category valuation account. Toggle use_location_valuation_account per product template to dictate priority.

Location Valuation Architecture

Per-Location Valuation Account Assignment (dn_valuation_account_id)

Assign specific General Ledger stock asset accounts directly to individual warehouses, assembly shops, or transit zones. The resolution engine performs a multi-tier fallback while respecting priority flags:

Hierarchical Upward Traversal If a child bin or shelf leaves the account blank, the resolver climbs parent locations (location_id) until a designated account is found.
Product Priority Switch Enable use_location_valuation_account on Product Template or Variant to give Location hierarchy precedence over the Product Category account.
Fail-Safe Validation If neither location nor category has an account assigned, the engine halts with an informative UserError to prevent corrupt unposted inventory.

Operational Workflow

How It Works in 4 Simple Steps

Seamlessly integrated into your daily inventory routine with zero disruption to warehouse staff:

Step 1

Configure Settings

Navigate to Inventory > Configuration > Settings. Choose your Move Modification Mode, Date Mode, and Decrease Cost Basis.

Step 2

Validate Transfers

Process standard receipts, internal transfers, and customer deliveries. The engine activates when pickings transition to the Done state.

Step 3

Auto-Post SVL & GL

The system automatically writes perpetual valuation layers and posts balanced journal entries according to your selected costing policy.

Step 4

Modify Done Moves

Update delivered quantities on completed moves when corrections occur. The system safely posts compensating SVLs or adjusts existing records.
